# Certification Pilot: One Page

WHAT YOU GET. A banded, envelope-explicit certificate of your
system's epistemic conduct on instruments it cannot game: per-band
accuracy, a stance decomposition separating entitled abstention
from error, and a fabrication rate for observations that do not
exist, every threshold registered before the run and every number
carrying provenance. Renewable per release, so the certificate
tracks your product, not a moment.

HOW IT RUNS. Three weeks. Week one: you run the public kit
against your endpoint yourself, on your infrastructure, nothing
leaves your machines; we agree bars in writing before any scored
run. Week two: scored runs on frozen, hash-published banks,
including never-published held-out items. Week three: certificate
issued with a joint read-out. If a bar is missed, the miss is
reported with mechanism analysis, never repositioned.

WHAT WE NEED. An endpoint or responses files, one technical
contact, and a decision on publication: certificates publish only
with your approval; misses stay private unless you choose
otherwise.

WHY US. The measurement path is public and self-verifying
(github.com/arcifact/arcifact-kit: hash-frozen banks, a scorer
whose parity is re-proven by CI on every commit, a runner you can
audit in one sitting). The method is preregistration and
adversarial gating, stated in docs/PROTOCOL.md. Patent pending,
GB2618664.3.
